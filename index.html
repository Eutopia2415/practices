To Autumn

John Keats

Vocabulary notes

thatch-eves: The edges of a roof made from straw or reeds.

granary: A building used to store grain.

winnowing: Separating grain from chaff with moving air.

gleaner: Someone who gathers grain left after reaping.

sallows: Willow trees.

bourn: A boundary or limit; here, a boundary of the hills.

garden-croft: A small enclosed plot or field adjoining a house.
